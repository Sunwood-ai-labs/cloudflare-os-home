# Design intent

This is a post-ready 3:2 social carousel, not an evidence-dashboard or memo. The headline leads with the result, each following slide has one action and one observed result, and the original UI screenshot remains the visual proof.

- Large editorial typography, short copy, and one dominant screenshot per slide.
- Cloudflare orange is a structural accent, not a faint decoration.
- Warm paper background, navy type, green proof state, and amber limitation state.
- No generic dashboard cards; use a strong split composition, oversized step numbers, and proof labels.
