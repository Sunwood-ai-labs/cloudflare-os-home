# Cloudflare OS Gadget Lab — Evidence Slides

## Direction

An evidence-first Japanese field report. Every slide answers two questions before the viewer studies the raw UI: 「何をしたか」 and 「何が分かったか」. Raw browser captures stay visible inside a clean editorial frame; explanatory copy never hides the evidence.

## Palette

- `#F4F6F8` — paper background
- `#0D1828` — navy rail and primary type
- `#F6821F` — Cloudflare action accent
- `#2AB78C` — confirmed result
- `#FFC857` — caution / failure / unverified boundary
- `#D7DEE7` — grid and rules

## Type

- Display: `Bricolage Grotesque`, fallback `Yu Gothic UI`
- Evidence labels: `IBM Plex Mono`, fallback `Cascadia Mono`
- Japanese copy is kept short, high contrast, and large enough to read in a social crop.

## Evidence rule

Do not redraw or beautify the captured UI. The screenshots are primary evidence. The left rail explains the action and result, while the screenshot frame preserves the original screen, including failures and limitations.
